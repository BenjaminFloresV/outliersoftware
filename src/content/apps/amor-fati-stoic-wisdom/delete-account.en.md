# Delete your account and data

**Amor Fati: Stoic Wisdom** · Developed by Outlier Software · Last updated: October 2, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Amor Fati account and the data you have stored in the cloud.

## From the app

In the app, go to **Profile > Delete my account**. You'll confirm with your Google account, and your account and your cloud data are deleted right away. You can also choose to delete the journal and progress on your phone. If you use the app on another phone, sign out there first; otherwise it could upload your journal again.

## By email

1. **Sign out of the app** on every phone where you use it (Profile > Sign out), or uninstall it. Otherwise, the app could upload what is still on your phone again after we delete it.
2. **Send an email** to [benjamin.floresjv@gmail.com](mailto:benjamin.floresjv@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20Amor%20Fati%20account) with the subject "Delete my Amor Fati account". Send it from the email address of the Google account you use in the app; that's how we find your account and confirm the request is yours. If you can't write from that address, tell us in the email and we will find another way to verify it's you.

We will delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm when it's done.

## What we delete

- **Your account:** the email address, name, profile photo link and user ID stored by Firebase Authentication.
- **Your progress** stored in the cloud: streak, rank and related settings.
- **Every journal entry and night exam** stored in the cloud.

We don't keep backup copies of the database, so once deleted, this data cannot be recovered.

## What is not deleted

- **Your subscription.** Deleting your account does not cancel Premium. Cancel it in Google Play, under [Payments & subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions).
- **The data on your phone,** unless you choose to delete them in the app. If you keep them, they stay on the phone without an account. If you ask us by email, they stay on the phone until you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings, and if you sign in again later, the app will treat them as another account's data: it will offer to remove them from the phone and won't upload them.
- **Your Android backup.** If backup is on, a copy of your progress and journal may be in your Google account's device backup. You manage it from your Google account.
- **Purchase records,** which Google Play keeps under its own policies.

For more details, see our [Privacy Policy](#/android/app/amor-fati-stoic-wisdom/privacy?lang=en).
