# Privacy Policy

**Amor Fati: Stoic Wisdom** · Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what information the Amor Fati: Stoic Wisdom app for Android ("the app") handles, where it is kept, who can see it and what you can do about it.

## Summary

- Most of what you do in the app stays on your phone.
- Your progress, journal and night exams are copied to the cloud only if you have Premium **and** you sign in with Google.
- The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.
- You can ask us to delete your account and your cloud data at any time.

Contact: [benjamin.floresjv@gmail.com](mailto:benjamin.floresjv@gmail.com)

## Information that stays on your phone

The app stores the following on your device:

- **Your progress:** your streak, the quotes you have already seen and your rank.
- **Your journal:** what you write about each day's quote.
- **Your night exams:** your answers to the three evening questions.
- **Your settings,** such as sound, reminders and the journal lock.

We cannot see any of this. It is not sent to us unless you use cloud backup (see the next section).

- **Android backup.** If backup is on in your phone's settings, Android copies your progress, journal, night exams and some settings to your Google account, so they can be restored on a new phone. Google stores that backup in your account. We cannot access it.
- **Journal lock.** If you lock your journal, the app asks Android to check your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Android does the check. The app never receives your biometric data or your screen lock.
- **Reminders.** Notifications are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us to show them. Some reminders show a short excerpt of a past journal entry.
- **Sharing and exporting.** When you share a quote as an image or export your journal as a PDF, the file is created on your phone and goes only where you choose to send or save it.

## Information stored in the cloud (Premium with Google sign-in)

If you have Premium and sign in with your Google account, the app keeps a copy of your data in the cloud so you can restore it on a new phone and use it on more than one device. We store:

- **Your account,** through Firebase Authentication: the email address, name and profile photo link of your Google account, and a user ID that Firebase assigns to you.
- **Your progress:** your streak and the date of your last visit, which quotes you have not seen yet and the last author shown, your highest rank, and two settings (sound on or off, and whether you have seen the tips).
- **Your journal:** for each day, the text you wrote and the quote it belongs to.
- **Your night exams:** for each day, your three answers.

This data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, on servers in the United States. It is saved under your user ID, and the database rules only let your signed-in account read or write it.

If your Premium ends, the app stops uploading new changes. What is already in the cloud stays there until you ask us to delete it.

## Purchases

Premium is a subscription sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or other payment details. The app only receives from Google Play whether your subscription is active, and keeps that on your phone. [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies to the purchase.

## Information handled by the services we use

- **Firebase Remote Config** lets us adjust some settings, such as the thresholds between ranks, without releasing an update. For this, the Firebase SDK creates a random installation identifier and sends it to Google along with technical details such as the app version, the Android version and the device language.
- **Google's servers receive your IP address** whenever the app connects to them, as with any internet service.
- **Google Play services** handle Google sign-in and purchases.

Google provides Firebase to us as our service provider (data processor), under the [Firebase data processing terms](https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms). You can read more in [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## What the app does not do

- It shows no ads.
- It uses no analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
- It does not access your location, contacts, camera or microphone.
- It does not use artificial intelligence to process your data.
- We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

## Who can see your cloud data

- **You,** on any phone where you sign in with the same Google account.
- **Google,** which hosts the database for us as our service provider.
- **The developer.** As the owner of the Firebase project, the developer can technically read the data in the cloud, because it is not end-to-end encrypted. We only access it to handle a request from you (for example, a support or deletion request) or when the law requires it.
- **Authorities,** only when the law requires us to disclose it.

Your data travels encrypted between your phone and Google's servers, and Google encrypts it at rest.

## Why we use your data (legal bases)

If the GDPR (European Union, United Kingdom) or the LGPD (Brazil) applies to you, these are our legal bases:

- **To provide the service you asked for** (performance of a contract): saving and syncing your data when you use cloud backup, and checking your subscription.
- **Our legitimate interests:** keeping the app working and secure, and adjusting its settings remotely.
- **Legal obligations,** such as answering requests from authorities.

We don't use your data for any other purpose, and we never use your journal or night exams to profile you.

## International transfers

Cloud data is stored in the United States. If you live elsewhere, your data is transferred there when you use cloud backup. Google protects these transfers with the safeguards the law requires, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## How long we keep your data

- **On your phone:** until you delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
- **In the cloud:** while your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it and your cloud data within 30 days. We don't keep backup copies of the database, so deleted data cannot be recovered.
- **Purchase records** are kept by Google Play under its own policies.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, receive a copy of it, object to or restrict how we use it, and complain to your data protection authority.

- You can see and edit your journal and night exams in the app at any time, and export them as a PDF.
- To delete your account and your cloud data, follow the steps in [How to delete your account](#/android/app/amor-fati-stoic-wisdom/delete-account?lang=en).
- For anything else, email us. We may ask you to write from your Google account's email address so we can confirm the request is yours. We will answer within 30 days.

If you live in California: we do not sell or share your personal information, as those terms are defined by the CCPA.

## Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from people under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

## Security

The database rules only let each account read and write its own data. Your phone is still the key to your journal: anyone who can unlock it can open the app, unless you use the journal lock. Protect your phone with a screen lock.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will let you know in advance by reasonable means, such as a notice in the app.
